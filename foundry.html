M / 001 · Manifesto

More than hiring. We cast engineering capability from India.

Hiring engineers is only the first step. The harder part is launching the team well, holding operating discipline, and scaling without losing quality. Senatio is a foundry for that capability — designed end-to-end, cast in weeks, run by operators.

The value is not raw headcount. The value is a managed system — sourcing, hiring, onboarding, payroll, delivery oversight, continuity, and scale — operating as one reactor from day one. The output is a team that performs reliably, holds shape under load, and grows on demand.
